How to play

Quick to learn. Better with a lively group.

Describe. Guess. Race to the finish.

The first team to reach the target score wins. Every correct guess earns one point.

A turn in three steps

  1. Describe

    The speaker explains the words on the card, in any order, before time runs out.

  2. Guess

    The listeners guess aloud. Keep trying: guesses are unlimited while the timer runs.

  3. Score & rotate

    Select the correctly guessed words, confirm the score, and pass to the next team. Rotate the speaker within your team each turn.

Give a great clue

You can

  • Use explanations, humming or gestures.
  • Describe the words in any order.
  • Say “and” or “the” if they appear in the answer.

Leave these out

  • The actual word or name, and related or derived words.
  • “Sounds like” or “rhymes with” clues.
  • Alphabet letters, including starting or ending letters.
  • Translations or other languages.
  • Pointing to objects in the room.
  • Acronyms, forwards or backwards.

Add a little Chaos

Earn your clue points, land, then resolve one effect. Boosts and steals help you move ahead; slips and bombs set you back. Bombs cost up to three points, and one shield blocks one penalty or incoming steal. Effects never chain.

The map changes each turn. Winning moves, Start and the tile before Finish have no effect. Find the full effect guide below the board during a Chaos game.

How Chaos works

Earn points, land, resolve one effect. The map changes every turn. Start, the tile before the finish, and winning moves have no effect. Effects do not chain. A zero-correct turn can still trigger an effect.

One shield blocks a landing penalty or an incoming steal, then is consumed. A blocked steal still gives the stealing team +1. Shields do not stack. Steal targets the leading opponent; ties are random.
